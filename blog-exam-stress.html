STUDENT WELLNESS GUIDE

Exam Stress Kaise Kam Karein — Evidence-Based Guide

VERIFIED SOURCES aneev AI · Student Wellness Series · ~6 min read

Board exams, semester exams, competitive exams — India ke students ke liye stress normal hai, lekin usse manage karna seekhna zaroori hai. Ye guide sirf "motivation" ki baatein nahi karti — har tip research aur official health sources par based hai (WHO, US CDC, US FDA, Govt of India helplines).

Exam stress ke common signs

  • Padhte waqt concentration tootna, baar-baar distract hona
  • Neend ka disturb hona — ya to neend nahi aati, ya bahut aati hai
  • pet mein jalan, acidity, ya khana chhut jana
  • Irritability — chhoti baat pe gussa ya rona
  • "Kuch yaad nahi ho raha" wali feeling — blank mind

1. Breaks lo — pomodoro-style study

25-50 minute ka focused study + 5-10 minute ka break. Break ke time phone scroll karna break nahi hai — walk karo, stretch karo, paani piyo, aankhein band karke aaram karo. Research mein spaced study with breaks, lambe baithke padhne se better memory consolidation dikhata hai.

2. 7-9 ghante ki neend — non-negotiable

US CDC ke according 18-25 saal ke young adults ko 7-9 hours neend chahiye. All-nighters recall ko kam karte hain, badhate nahi — kyunki memory consolidation neend ke dauran hoti hai. Fixed sleep-wake time rakho (weekend pe bhi), kamra dark-cool-quiet rakho, aur sone se 1 ghanta pehle screens band karo.

3. Roz 20-30 minute exercise ya walk

Physical activity cortisol (stress hormone) ko girati hai. Gym ki zaroorat nahi — tez walk, cycling, ya ghar pe skipping bhi kaam karta hai. Baith ke padhne wale har ghante mein 3-5 minute uth ke chal aao.

4. Caffeine ki limit samjho

US FDA ke according healthy adults ke liye max 400 mg/day caffeine (approx 3-4 cup coffee ya 5-6 cup chai). Raat ko energy drinks aur coffee avoid karo — anxiety worsen hoti hai aur neend kharab hoti hai. Overdose ke signs: jitters, tez dhadakta dil, haath kaanpna.

5. Practice exams — sabse underrated trick

Time-bound mock tests real exam anxiety ko significantly kam karte hain — research-backed hai. Kyun? Kyunki exam ka pressure practice mein "normal" ho jaata hai. Exam se pehle kam se kam 3-5 full-length mocks in exam-like conditions (timer laga ke, phone door).

6. Khana skip mat karo

Breakfast skip karne se concentration girta hai. Protein roz lo — dal, chana, rajma, eggs, soya, dahi — sab saste options hain. Sugar-heavy drinks aur junk se energy crash hota hai. Paani dhyaan se pio — halka dehydration bhi mood aur focus kharab karta hai.

7. Perspective rakho — ek exam zindagi nahi hai

Stress ka ek bada reason: "sab ispe depend hai" wala thought. Honest sach: har saal lakhs log exam dete hain, aur life mein kai raste hote hain. Apne se compare mat karo, sirf apne kal se compare karo.

Kab professional help lena zaroori hai

Agar 2+ hafte se ye signs hain — roz udas mood, kuch mein interest nahi, hopelessness, panic attacks, ya khud ko nuksan pahunchane ke thoughts — to ye sirf "exam stress" nahi hai. Free aur confidential madad 24/7 available hai:

  • Tele-MANAS: 14416 — Govt of India, free, 20+ languages
  • KIRAN: 1800-599-0019
  • Kisi bharosemand insaan se baat karo — parent, teacher, counsellor

Mental health problem hone mein sharam ki baat nahi — ye treatable hai.

Quick recap: breaks + 7-9 ghante neend + roz exercise + caffeine limit + practice exams + proper khana + perspective. Bas — koi jaadu nahi, bas consistency.
